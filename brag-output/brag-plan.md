# Brag Plan: Rotaract Club of NIBM

## What is this app?
An interactive, cyber-purple digital headquarters for the Rotaract Club of NIBM (Rotary International District 3220), featuring instant event registration passes, annual board succession governance, a public community blog, and real-time leadership portals.

## The angle
Campus youth leadership isn't just about meetings—it's high-octane community impact, fellowship, and decentralized digital governance. This video treats Rotaract Club NIBM like a premier modern tech launch, revealing its glowing visual aesthetic, live digital passes, board leadership succession, and presidential publishing studio.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep cyber-purple cosmic gradient with glowing flowing curved neon lines and ambient particle bursts. A bold central declaration slams in:
**"YOUTH LEADERSHIP. COMMUNITY IMPACT. BEYOND CAMPUS."**
Followed immediately by the Rotaract Club of NIBM gold & purple crest.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1: Instant Event Discovery & Digital Event Passes (2.5s - 7.0s)**
  Showcase high-energy initiatives (Cancer Awareness Run 2026, Rugby Clash, Leadership Summit) transitioning into a live interactive VIP attendee pass with verified QR code and admission timestamp.
- **Moment 2: 3D Leadership Matrix & Annual Board Succession (7.0s - 12.0s)**
  Card reveal of Executive Board directors (President, VP, Secretary) with glowing neon aura borders, highlighting the decentralized Annual Board Succession tool that lets leaders transfer portfolios.
- **Moment 3: The Newsroom & Presidential Editorial Studio (12.0s - 16.5s)**
  The newly launched official Blog: category pills (*Leadership*, *Community Service*, *Environment*), reading modal, and the exclusive presidential publishing studio.

## Outro / punchline (16.5s - 20.0s)
The camera zooms back out to full screen. The official white Rotaract NIBM badge glows with a neon aura:
**"ROTARACT CLUB OF NIBM"**
**"United in Leadership, Service & Innovation"**
Punchline CTA:
`rotaractnibm.org • Rotary District 3220`

## User flow worth showing
1. Browse upcoming community initiatives → Generate immediate verified attendee pass.
2. Executive leadership portal → Seamless annual role transfer & governance.
3. President authoring studio → Instant broadcast of official dispatches to the world.
