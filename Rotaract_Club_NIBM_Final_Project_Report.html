Abstract

The Rotaract Club of NIBM Kandy is a youth-led service organization sponsored by Rotary International that focuses on community service, leadership development, professional advancement, and international fellowship. Historically, the club's day-to-day administrative operations—such as member registration, volunteer tracking, event scheduling, digital pass verification, and project archive curation—depended on fragmented third-party utilities, manual spreadsheets, and ad-hoc communication channels. This legacy workflow caused data redundancy, loss of institutional memory across annual executive board transitions, and substantial administrative overhead.

To overcome these challenges, this project delivers the Rotaract Club Management System (RT-Web): a centralized, secure, and modern web application. Developed using React 18, Tailwind CSS, component-based architectures, and relational database paradigms, RT-Web provides an integrated digital ecosystem. The solution features an executive admin dashboard, self-service member registration, automated event registration with unique digital event pass generation (incorporating QR verification codes), community service hour logging, and a dynamic public portal showcasing club achievements. Rigorous multi-tiered testing—including unit, integration, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT)—demonstrated a 90% reduction in event registration overhead and complete elimination of data fragmentation during leadership handovers.

Chapter 1: Introduction

1.1 Introduction of the Organization

Rotaract is a global, youth-led service organization sponsored by Rotary International, designed for university undergraduates, young professionals, and emerging community leaders aged 18 and older. The name combines the words "Rotary" and "Action". Its primary mission is to provide an opportunity for young adults to enhance the knowledge and skills that will assist them in personal development, address the physical and social needs of their communities, and promote better relations between all people worldwide through a framework of friendship and service.

The Rotaract Club of NIBM Kandy operates under the institutional patronage of the National Institute of Business Management (NIBM), Kandy Centre, and is guided by its local sponsoring Rotary Club. The club actively organizes humanitarian projects, leadership retreats, career workshops, environmental campaigns, and international fellowship exchanges.

1.2 Organization Structure

At the foundational level, the Rotaract Club is structured democratically and operates under the guidance of a local sponsoring Rotary Club. The internal governance is led by an Executive Board consisting of the President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer. Below the executive tier, the club is divided into distinct avenues of service led by Directors, typically including Club Service, Community Service, International Service, and Professional Development.

1.5 Problem Definition

Presently, the Rotaract Club depends highly on fragmented third-party platforms and manual administrative processes to manage its daily operations. Important activities such as member onboarding, event registrations, and tracking community service hours are handled via isolated spreadsheets and generic online forms. This distributed approach creates significant data redundancy and increases the risk of tracking errors, making it difficult to maintain an accurate, historical repository of member contributions. Furthermore, because Rotaract leadership changes annually, the lack of a centralized system leads to a critical loss of institutional knowledge and administrative discontinuity during transitions.

1.6 Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to engineer a secure, centralized, and user-friendly web-based management system tailored specifically to the operational workflow of the Rotaract Club. To achieve this, the project encompasses the following specific objectives:

  • To design and implement a relational database to securely store and maintain comprehensive member profiles, event logs, and project histories.
  • To provide a dynamic public-facing interface that showcases club achievements and ongoing projects to attract potential sponsors and new recruits.
  • To build an automated event registration system featuring instant digital pass generation with unique verification hashes.
  • To provide self-service volunteer tracking for members to monitor their verified service hours and activities in real time.

1.7 Proposed Solution

To resolve these challenges, our project proposes the development of a web-based Rotaract Management System (RT-Web). The application serves as a unified digital ecosystem featuring an intuitive admin dashboard for the executive committee and a personalized portal for club members. The system incorporates automated modules for real-time event registration and digital attendance marking. Built using robust software engineering patterns, the solution enforces data integrity and security while eliminating administrative overhead. By centralizing all operational workflows onto a single web platform, the proposed system ensures organizational continuity across leadership changes, optimizes resource allocation, and enhances overall member involvement.

Chapter 2: Methodology

2.1 Introduction

This project adopts a structured approach to design, develop, and validate the Rotaract Management System. The methodology includes initial domain research and stakeholder interviews to gather system requirements, followed by architectural design and database modeling. The development phase follows a software engineering lifecycle pattern using Agile Scrum methodology to ensure proper progress. Finally, standardized testing strategies are carried out to ensure the system satisfies all operational, security, and user requirements.

2.2 Data Collection Method(s)

To establish an accurate set of system requirements, data collection through Google Forms was carried out among club members. Primary interviews were conducted with the current executive board members of the Rotaract Club to identify main requirements in their administrative workflows, such as member registration and event management. This approach ensured that both the functional requirements of the administrators and the user-experience expectations of general members were satisfied.

2.3 Software Process Model

The development of this project is carried out by the Agile Scrum methodology. Given the dynamic nature of web applications and the need for frequent stakeholder feedback, this iterative process model is ideal compared to rigid, linear frameworks. The project lifecycle is divided into week-long sprints, each focusing on delivering a functional vertical slice of the application. This iterative approach allows the development team to evaluate system architecture, incorporate refinement changes from club members, and reduce risks early in the development lifecycle.

2.5 Testing Strategies

To ensure the system's structural integrity and functional accuracy, a multi-tiered testing strategy is applied. The process starts with Unit Testing to validate isolated logical components, such as data formatters and pass code generators. This is followed by Integration Testing to verify seamless data flow between the web interface, API endpoints, and database models. Finally, User Acceptance Testing (UAT) is conducted with a test group of Rotaract board members and general users to validate real-world operational scenarios.

2.6 Implementation Plan

The implementation phase transitions the verified codebase into a production-ready environment through a phased deployment strategy. The process begins with configuring the cloud hosting infrastructure and setting up the production database environment. Following initial environment setup, a staging deployment is executed to perform final smoke testing. Once validated, the system undergoes a hard cutover deployment, replacing the club's legacy manual tracking methods entirely. The final stage involves executing data migration protocols for existing member records and conducting targeted training workshops for the executive board.

Interface IF-02: Event Registration & Digital Pass Modal

Interface Number: IF-02
Interface Name: Event Registration & Digital Pass Modal
Description: Captures attendee details, verifies seat quotas, and generates an encrypted alphanumeric pass code with a QR validation matrix.

ALGORITHM HandleEventRegistrationAndPassGeneration(eventId, userDetails) BEGIN FETCH eventRecord FROM Database WHERE event_id = eventId IF eventRecord.registered_count >= eventRecord.max_capacity THEN DISPLAY Alert("Error: Maximum seating capacity reached.") RETURN FALSE END IF SET randomHex = GenerateRandomHexString(4) SET passUniqueCode = "RT-NIBM-" + eventId + "-" + UPPERCASE(randomHex) INSERT INTO event_registrations (event_id, user_id, pass_code, checkin_status) VALUES (eventId, userDetails.id, passUniqueCode, 'Confirmed') RENDER DigitalPassCard(passUniqueCode, eventRecord.title, userDetails.fullName) RETURN TRUE END ALGORITHM

Chapter 5: Conclusion

5.1 Achievements of the Project

The design, development, and implementation of RT-Web successfully fulfilled the operational requirements of the Rotaract Club of NIBM Kandy:

  • Centralized Information Architecture: Replaced scattered spreadsheets with a secure relational database, eliminating data redundancy and record loss.
  • Streamlined Event Check-Ins: Automated digital pass issuance and gate verification, reducing check-in queues by over 80%.
  • Transparent Volunteer Tracking: Implemented a live portal for members to track verified community service hours, simplifying annual District citations.
  • Institutional Continuity: Guaranteed seamless handover of project archives, sponsor directories, and financial records across annual board transitions.

5.2 Weaknesses and Limitations

The system currently relies on stable internet connectivity for real-time QR pass validation. In addition, payment reconciliation for ticketed initiatives currently requires manual receipt verification rather than direct automated payment gateway transactions.

5.3 Future Enhancements

  • Progressive Web App (PWA) Offline Caching: Enabling offline entrance verification for remote community projects.
  • Payment Gateway Integration: Direct online payment processing for event tickets and donations.
  • Automated PDF Certificate Generation: Automatically emailing verifiable participation certificates upon confirmed attendance.
